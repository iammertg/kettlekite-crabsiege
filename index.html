How it plays

Drag anywhere to run, and the rest happens on its own: the captain chops, collects and swings.

  1. Chop and carry

    Your captain chops palms and scoops up the gold the crabs drop. It all piles up on their back.

  2. Build where it says

    Stand on a dashed square and the stack pours in. A watchtower, a gate or a cannon pops up.

  3. Hold the line

    Crabs march up the sand path in waves. Clear the last one and the stage is yours, for up to three stars.

A cove worth holding.

Towers and cannons for the beach, a gate for the path, and a King Crab stomping ashore every few stages.

Watchtowers

Bamboo towers rain stones on the crabs. Build one on its square, then upgrade it as the waves grow.

Cannons

A cannon hits a whole crowd at once, so it earns its place where the crabs bunch up.

The gate

A gate shuts the path. The crabs have to stop and bash it, and your captain can walk straight through.

The King Crab

Every few stages a giant, crowned crab comes up the beach. Bring your best gate.

Easy to pick up.

Short stages you can play with one thumb, with plenty of strategy in what you build first.

Get stronger

Clear stages to unlock permanent captain perks. Start a stage with a Treasure Chest of coins, or call a Cannon Barrage or a Coconut Rush when a wave gets wild.

Grow the cove

Dig a gold mine and open a fish grill: villagers queue up and pay in coins. Hire a helper to keep the grill running while you fight, and clear the fog to open new land.

Come back each day

Daily gifts and a Tiki Streak for winning in a row. Climb the Lighthouse or race rafts across the lagoon in events, and earn rewards on the Treasure Pass.

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Purchases go through the App Store, where Screen Time and Ask to Buy can limit them.
