How it plays

Drag anywhere to run, and the rest happens on its own: the captain chops, collects and swings.

  1. Build where it says

    Stand still on a priced square and what you carry pours in. A watchtower, a gate or a cannon pops up.

  2. Head out past the fence

    Palms, gold rocks and crab nests wait outside the camp. A nest pays in sailors or a sharper cutlass, and lost islanders you walk home go to work for you.

  3. Hold the gate

    Crabs march up the road in waves. Archers shoot from the towers and the captain swings at the gate. Clear the last wave, for up to three stars.

Every crab has its answer.

From stage 8, every stage tells you who is coming, so you know what to build first. Each island ends with the King Crab.

  • Leapers

    They jump the gate. A Gate Net stops them.

  • Hermits

    Arrows bounce off their shells. A gun or the Boiling Pot gets through.

  • Runners

    Fast. Road Spikes slow them down.

  • Bombers

    They blow up the gate. Shoot them on the road.

  • The Swarm

    Small and many. Cannons and the Boiling Pot.

  • The King Crab

    Huge, and crowned. A Harpoon or a gun hits hardest.

A cove worth holding.

Towers and cannons for the road, a gate across it, and more to build as the islands get harder.

Watchtowers

Sailors shoot from the platform. Upgrade one and you choose: three archers for a crowd, or one gun that cracks shells.

Cannons

A cannon hits a whole crowd at once, so it earns its place where the crabs bunch up.

The gate

A gate shuts the road, and the crabs have to stop and bash it. Hang a net on it when Leapers come.

The Boiling Pot

It scalds every crab at the gate and clears the crowd for you.

Easy to pick up.

Short stages you can play with one thumb, with plenty of strategy in what you build first.

Get stronger

Clear stages to unlock permanent captain perks. Tap Rally and every tower shoots twice as fast for eight seconds, or call a Cannon Barrage or a Coconut Rush when a wave gets wild.

Grow the cove

Dig a gold mine and open a fish grill: villagers queue up and pay in coins. Bring home a lost miner, fish seller or lumberjack to work while you fight, and open new land when the camp gets tight.

Come back each day

Daily gifts and a Tiki Streak for winning in a row. Climb the Lighthouse or race rafts across the lagoon in events, and earn rewards on the Treasure Pass.

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone; Game Center, if you use it, sees only the stage you have reached. Purchases go through the App Store, where Screen Time and Ask to Buy can limit them.
