Support

Crab Siege, an island-defense game for iPhone.

Write to support@kettlekite.com. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened, and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I move, chop and fight?

Drag anywhere on the screen and the captain runs that way. Chopping palm trees, swinging the cutlass and picking up coins all happen on their own when you are close enough.

How do I build?

Stand on a dashed square. The coins or wood you carry pour into it, and when the price shown on the square has been paid, the building pops up. Standing on an arrow square upgrades what is already there.

What makes me win or lose a stage?

You win when every wave has come and no crab is left on the beach. You lose if the hut in the middle falls. If that happens, Keep Going lets you carry on: the hut is mended, the gates are repaired and the crabs on the field are swept away.

What do the boosters do?

The Cannon Barrage hits every crab on the field at once. The Coconut Rush makes the captain faster for a short while. The Treasure Chest starts a stage with extra coins.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your stages, stars, gems and boosters, and purchases can’t be restored after that.

I bought something but it didn’t arrive

Close the game and open it again: the App Store delivers any purchase that didn’t finish the next time the game starts. If it still isn’t there, write to us.

How do I stop purchases on a child’s iPhone?

Use Screen Time (iOS Settings, then Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions) or Ask to Buy in Family Sharing.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
